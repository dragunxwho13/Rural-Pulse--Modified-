How RuralPulse works

A care-navigation aid, not a diagnosis tool. Rules decide urgency; AI only helps understand language and can only ever escalate.

  1. Danger signs first16 red-flag rules run on the raw words before anything else, even offline. A match shows 108 at once.
  2. UnderstandOffline vocabulary for 14 languages. With a Gemini key, AI helps with the rest, under a strict schema. It can only add, never remove, a danger sign.
  3. Read back"You said… I understood… Is this right?" in the patient's language. Only missing facts are asked.
  4. Versioned urgency rulesEmergency, today, 2-3 days, self-care, or "I can't safely decide". Children, older adults, pregnancy and long-term illness raise the level.
  5. Nearest right placeOpenStreetMap health centres near the patient, government first, hospitals only for emergencies, with fastest and main-road routes.
  6. Doctor gets readyThe ASHA sends the case; the nearest doctor's dashboard rings with symptoms and a prep list. The doctor's medicine card reaches the patient in their language.
  • Never names a disease. Symptom checkers get the first diagnosis right only 19–38% of the time.
  • Never gives medicine doses. Only the doctor's prescription does, shown exactly as written.
  • AI cannot lower urgency. Patient edits cannot remove a danger sign either.
  • Says when it is unsure. Untested language, missing age or duration: hand-over to the ASHA or 104.
  • Shows where data comes from. Verified records, OpenStreetMap places and listed doctors are labelled differently.
  • Ignores hidden instructions. "Ignore previous instructions" is detected and logged.

Supported

Telehealth works across rural contexts; triage is more reliable than diagnosis; most eSanjeevani use (93%) is assisted by health workers.

Promising, untested here

Read-back confirmation, hybrid facility matching and AI translation for low-resource languages: RuralPulse is built to measure them.

Not claimed

That it saves lives or money, handles any dialect, or that OSM and listing data are current. These are hypotheses to test.
